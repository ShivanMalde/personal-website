About

Shivan Malde

I'm a London based DevOps engineer and I would love to help you with your project. I have 6 years of experience in the software industry, the last 4 were in a dedicated DevOps role. I specialise in automation and optimisation of the software development life-cycle through CI/CD using tools such as Terraform and Azure DevOps. I can design, implement, and maintain infrastructure on cloud platforms such as Azure, with particular focus on Docker and Kubernetes. I am passionate about using technology to enhance efficiency, reliability, and security of software systems.
